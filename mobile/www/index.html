Hikasha Chat

Multistream chat, in your pocket.

Paste your BotRix chat widget link once. The app remembers it on this device and opens the chat directly next time.

How to get your BotRix chat link
  1. Open botrix.live in your phone browser and sign in to your BotRix account.
  2. Open your dashboard and link the accounts you stream to: Twitch, YouTube, and Kick.
  3. Go to Widgets and choose the Chat widget (multistream chat).
  4. Copy the chat widget link. It must include ?bid=....
  5. Return to Hikasha Chat, paste the link above, and tap Connect.

If you change your BotRix widget later, copy the new widget link and use the gear button in chat to update it here.

Private link. The ?bid= value is a session secret. Do not share it. It is stored only on this device and is never sent anywhere by this app.
